Supplies for Dreams

University of Chicago Chapter

At Supplies For Dreams, we connect students in the Chicago Public School system with college mentors and resources that expand their horizons. Through weekly mentorship, field trips, and scholarship resources, we help to foster confidence and curiosity for students to pursue their dream careers.

About Us

Since 2019 we have developed career-oriented programming for students in partnership with Talcott Elementary. The University of Chicago already provides CPS students with a range of academically focused mentorship programs: NSP in-school programs, Maroon Tutor Match, and Homework Help Centers support students in the classroom with quality school tutoring. At the University of Chicago Chapter of Supplies for Dreams, we supplement these academic tutoring programs and in-class learning with non-academic but similarly forward-thinking programs to develop students' curiosity in career options and confidence to pursue their gaols. Through weekly mentorship programs, we introduce a range of career options to students and educate them on practical steps necessary to pursue these careers. In field trips to academic centers, we incorporate learning into fun excursions and highlight jobs in the arts and biology that might otherwise be overlooked. We also provide resources on scholarship opportunities and outlined career paths to parents of our students, which are also available online here at our website.
